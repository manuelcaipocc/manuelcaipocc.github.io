Machine Learning Engineer combining a mechanical engineering foundation with graduate training in numerical methods and machine learning theory, focused on physics-aware and reliable AI for complex cyber-physical systems. Strong ability to interpret sensor behavior, analyze physical degradation mechanisms, and translate them into physically consistent models for anomaly detection and remaining useful life prediction, deployed end-to-end on high-frequency time-series data with demonstrated industrial impact. My current research extends this foundation toward agentic AI, developing LLM-based agents that orchestrate specialized ML models across signals, logs, and engineering artifacts, together with knowledge distillation methods that turn historical cases and validated expert decisions into reusable and verifiable reasoning. The goal is explainable and reliable AI that accelerates engineering processes, reduces manual analysis effort, and keeps every decision traceable to its physical and data evidence.
